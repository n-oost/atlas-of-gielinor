## Separation of mechanics vs marker placement

Per project instructions, settled text and mechanic corrections were strictly separated from unresolved marker placement:
- For `poi_005888`, `poi_005858`, and `poi_005862`, cross-catalog analysis confirmed that these records duplicate pre-existing canonical quest markers (`poi_003672` at `2780, 3087, 1`, `poi_001426` at `3191, 3362, 0`, and `poi_001791` at `2715, 3482, 0`). Their authored coordinates (`2900, 3500, 0`, `3074, 3515, 0`, `2740, 3445, 0`) reflect nearby regions or later quest stages rather than initial quest start spawns. In accordance with guidelines, their coordinates were **preserved without relocation** pending formal deduplication or removal approval.
- For `poi_005871` and `poi_005883`, authored coordinates are situated at target destination regions (`2185, 3145, 0` at Tyras Camp and `2840, 3920, 0` at Weiss approach) rather than the NPC start givers (King Lathas in Ardougne Castle and Burntmeat in Troll Stronghold kitchen). Their coordinates were preserved without relocation while their metadata was corrected.
- For `poi_002112` and `poi_007113`, the coordinates at Lake Molch (`1371, 3632, 0` and `1370, 3632, 0`) accurately target Lake Molch aerial fishing. The tools and dual skill requirements were updated from generic fishing to aerial fishing.
- For `poi_005778`, explicit user instructions authorized removal of the false overworld marker, relying on canonical under-cavern marker `poi_006365`.
- For sailing island hunter spots (`poi_005808`, `poi_005810`, `poi_005791`, `poi_006968`, `poi_002604`), live server teleports established the exact resident creature species and level requirements, replacing generic placeholders with factual creature details.
